AI Corral

Can you make the AI buck?

AI models are probabilistic. The systems around them do not have to be.

AI Corral demonstrates how deterministic rules can validate, reject, and retry model output before it is allowed through.

    Why this exists

    The useful work around the model

    The interesting part of AI engineering often happens outside the model. AI Corral is a small demonstration of treating model output as untrusted input until the surrounding system verifies that it satisfies explicit rules.
