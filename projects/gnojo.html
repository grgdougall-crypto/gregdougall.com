Control model / 02

AI with boundaries

Project evolution / 04

From answers to integrity

Gnojo started as a guided troubleshooting system. As the project grew, the harder problem became maintaining the knowledge itself: detecting broken relationships, stale structure, missing provenance, and repair decisions that needed human review.

Guided Troubleshooting

The project became less about producing answers and more about keeping the underlying knowledge dependable.
