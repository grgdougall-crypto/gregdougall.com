V1 product story

Routine preparation can move forward under bounded automation. Human judgment governs the consequential gates, and insufficient evidence produces a clear stop rather than an invented answer.

Operator experience / 01

Prepare → Review → Complete

A complex research and authoring pipeline is presented as three meaningful operator steps.

01

Prepare

Build from evidence

Gnojo researches governed sources, verifies evidence, prepares claims, and assembles a workflow proposal. It advances routine work only while the next step is safe and eligible.

02

Review

Focus human judgment

A reviewer inspects the evidence-backed proposal and resolves only the source, evidence, claim, or safety decisions that require judgment.

03

Complete

Add validated work

Validated work is explicitly added to the library and versioned. Draft approval and publication remain separate, human-authorized decisions.

Application walkthrough / 02

From opportunity to governed outcome

Real views from Gnojo v1 show both a workflow that reached publication and a workflow that stopped when its evidence could not support completion.

Governance / 03

Automation with a stopping rule

AI assists with preparation and routine progression. It does not approve its own consequential decisions or publish independently.

01

Bounded assistance

Research triage, evidence classification, claim preparation, and proposal assembly operate within explicit scopes.

02

Human authority

Source approval, genuine exceptions, draft creation, and publication remain governed human decisions.

03

Traceable support

Sources, evidence, claims, and generated artifacts retain provenance and stable identities.

04

Current-state checks

Fingerprints and stale-state validation prevent an old review from authorizing changed work.

05

Deterministic validation

Structure, coverage, safety, and publication readiness are checked before governed boundaries can be crossed.
