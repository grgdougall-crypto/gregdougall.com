Control model / 03

AI assists the report, not the operation

AI helps explain patterns, summarize operational conditions, and organize findings. It does not execute operational actions or make decisions on the user's behalf.

AI can
  • Summarize structured information
  • Explain operational patterns
  • Organize risk findings
  • Produce concise report language
Report supportHuman decisions stay outside the modelAnalysis only
AI cannot
  • Change source data
  • Execute operational actions
  • Approve decisions
  • Operate independently
